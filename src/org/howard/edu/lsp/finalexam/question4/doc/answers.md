Over the semester, my perspective on using AI in programming has become more structured and intentional. At first I treated it mainly as a fast way to generate code or fix errors, but I quickly realized that this approach could lead to shallow understanding and missed bugs. As I worked through larger assignments, I started using AI more as a support tool for improving the quality of my code rather than simply producing it. I began focusing on how it helps me identify edge cases, improve logic flow, and suggest cleaner implementations that I may not have considered initially. This made me more aware of readability, efficiency, and correctness in my own programming decisions.

In future programming and coursework, I plan to use AI as a tool for refining and strengthening my code rather than replacing the thinking process. I will use it to review my solutions for potential improvements, such as reducing complexity, improving structure, and catching logical errors that are easy to overlook. I also plan to use it to compare different approaches to the same problem so I can better understand trade offs between efficiency and simplicity. However, I will ensure that I can fully explain and implement my solutions independently before relying on AI suggestions.
